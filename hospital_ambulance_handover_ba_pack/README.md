## Implementation positioning

The pack demonstrates business analysis leadership across discovery, stakeholder mapping, requirements definition, current/future process design, SOP development, RACI, escalation design, training planning, KPI design, feedback reporting, and benefits realisation.

KPI figures, stakeholder records, and feedback summaries should be validated with hospital data-owner approval before external circulation, executive sign-off, or procurement use.
